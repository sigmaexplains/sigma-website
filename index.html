Why Sigma exists

AI that gives answers can make students worse at the test.

In a large field study, high-school students using a general AI chatbot scored much higher on practice problems and lower on the exam they later took alone. A tutor built to guide instead of answer removed most of that harm.

So we measure the thing that counts: whether you can solve a new problem on your own, days later.

+48%
practice scores with an answer-giving AI
−17%
on the exam taken without it

Bastani et al., “Generative AI without guardrails can harm learning”, PNAS, 2025

How it works

Courses, a tutor and mastery, in one place

  1. 1

    Learn in the right order

    Every course is a map of concepts, from beginner to advanced. Each lesson starts with where the idea shows up, then why it works, then what it is: Where → Why → What.

  2. 2

    Get unstuck, not handed the answer

    Ask Sigma answers from the course you're in. It asks what you've tried and gives one small hint at a time, so you do the thinking.

  3. 3

    Prove it on your own

    A short quiz checks each concept, without the tutor. Your progress shows what you've mastered and what to learn next.
